## A client brief, taken all the way to release

Lagnam Matrimony is a matrimonial Android app developed for Smart Space Technologies. This was paid freelance work, delivered in March 2026 in approximately 15 days. The client owns the product; I independently handled its development and release.

## My contribution

I worked from the client's requirements through implementation, testing, and deployment. My scope included the React Native application, authentication, backend/API work, Razorpay payment gateway integration, and Google Play submission.

## Learning a new platform while shipping

I came into the project with React and full-stack development experience, but without prior React Native experience. I used AI-assisted development extensively to learn the framework and implement the app, working through the platform-specific pieces required for release.

## The release details that mattered

Two areas needed particular attention: configuring the Play Store signing-key identifiers for authentication, and resolving Razorpay integration issues. Both were addressed as part of getting the application ready for production.

## Outcome

The app was released on Google Play. It was a complete client delivery: requirements, implementation, payment integration, and deployment. Product marketing and subsequent business operations remained with the client.
